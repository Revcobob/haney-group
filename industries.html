Clients

Industries we serve.

The firm represents organizations whose Texas priorities cannot afford to be misunderstood — nine sectors where our work is concentrated, and a trusted roster of associations, providers, and operators who rely on principal-level representation when the stakes are real.

Healthcare

Managed care, hospitals, physician groups, and Medicaid policy. The firm supports clients across health care reimbursement, scope of practice, and access policy.

Local Government

Cities, counties, special districts, and the recurring preemption questions that shape what local entities can and cannot do.

Infrastructure & Water

Capital appropriations, permitting, and the regulators of the next decade. Long-cycle issues that require interim and session work in equal measure.

Real Estate & Land Use

Property rights, housing policy, and association-led advocacy. Sector posture that has to balance member alignment with achievable legislation.

Economic Development

Incentive programs, regional priorities, and statewide growth policy. Including the interim work that determines what is even introduced.

Regulated Industries

High-visibility issues that turn on member relationships and disciplined communications across the session arc.

Education

Higher education, public schools, and the budget riders that decide policy in practice. Communications discipline for issues that arrive with public attention.

Public Policy & Procedure

Associations, coalitions, and policy organizations preparing for session — and for institutional process work on how chambers operate.

Transportation

Transportation issues across the Capitol and the agencies that implement them, including funding, automobiles, infrastructure, and commuter rail.
